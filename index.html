Six simple steps

From bare to brilliant.

Everything you need for a neat, long-lasting finish—without leaving home.

01

Prep

Wash and dry hands, remove polish, push back cuticles and wipe nails clean.

02

Choose

Match each nail with a wrap. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller one.

03

Peel

Lift the wrap carefully from its backing without touching the sticky side.

04

Apply

Place just above the cuticle, then press from the centre out to smooth it down.

05

Trim

Fold the excess over the nail tip and file gently downwards in one direction.

06

Seal

Press around every edge. Add a clear top coat after 30–60 minutes for extra wear.

Jenelle’s tip

Apply to completely clean, dry nails and avoid water or heavy cleaning for the first hour.

♥

Keep them looking fresh

Caring for your wraps

  1. Avoid water, hot showers, swimming and heavy cleaning for at least one hour after application.
  2. Wear gloves when using cleaning products.
  3. Reapply a clear top coat every few days to help extend wear.

When you’re ready

Gentle removal

  1. Soak in coconut oil or use nail polish remover, then gently lift one edge with a cuticle stick or fingernail.
  2. Slowly peel the wrap away without forcing it.
  3. If needed, work a little cuticle oil or remover around the edges to release the adhesive.
